We present LaST-HD, a human-to-robot action-learning framework that aligns unpaired human-hand and robot demonstrations in a shared latent physical-reasoning space. An action-conditioned world model, the low-cost OOL Glove, and a progressive mixed-to-human training recipe enable data-efficient transfer and rapid adaptation across robot embodiments.
