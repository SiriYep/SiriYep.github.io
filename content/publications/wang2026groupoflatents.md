We propose Group-of-Latents (GoL), a generative video compression framework that separates intra-frame perceptual anchors from inter-frame synthesis in a causal latent space. By combining a compact deep compression module with a DiT-based unified latent denoiser, the method achieves state-of-the-art perceptual fidelity below 0.005 bpp while preserving spatial detail and temporal consistency.
