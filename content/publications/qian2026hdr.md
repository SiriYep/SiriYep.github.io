We present HDR, a hierarchical denoising framework that organizes video latents into a coarse-to-fine tree for globally consistent, low-latency multi-step visual reasoning. HDR substantially improves reasoning success and progress over streaming autoregressive baselines, runs 54.2 times faster than bidirectional diffusion, and transfers to physical robot interaction and world-action modeling.
