We present EchoArena, an evaluator-centric world model for reliable closed-loop evaluation of VLA policies. By combining task-agnostic kinematics pretraining, failure-inclusive data, and joint-space proprioceptive conditioning, it evaluates five policies across ten tasks and three embodiments while producing rankings that better align with real-world outcomes.
