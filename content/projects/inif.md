Co-authored INIF, an open-source research project for controllable biological microscopy image compression using implicit neural representations.

## Highlights

- Contributed data collection and curation, biomedical application guidance, and research communication for the project.
- Supports arbitrary-dimensional microscopy images with pixel-, region-, and multi-resolution decoding.
- Combines implicit neural representations, learned optimization, codec priors, and task-specific guidance.
- Enables controllable compression ratios up to 512× while preserving downstream analytical utility.
