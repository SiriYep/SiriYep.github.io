An independently designed and maintained Zotero plugin that keeps Zotero items, paper PDFs, and Obsidian literature notes connected through bidirectional links.

## Highlights

- Creates or updates Obsidian literature notes directly from Zotero items and PDF attachments.
- Preserves handwritten note content while refreshing managed metadata, Zotero links, and Obsidian links.
- Supports configurable filename templates and collision-safe batch normalization for existing notes.
- Ships a bilingual interface, installable XPI releases, an update manifest, and a companion Agent Skill for structured paper reading.
