Co-authored LaST-R1 and contributed to its official open-source implementation for reinforcement-learning post-training of latent-reasoning VLA policies.

## Highlights

- Co-developed the official codebase through the team's shared development workflow.
- Implements LAPO training on Qwen3-VL and veRL, covering environment rollout, distributed training, and policy optimization.
- Releases warm-up SFT and post-RL checkpoints with training and evaluation configurations for all LIBERO suites.
- Demonstrates the framework on four real-world single-arm and dual-arm manipulation tasks.
