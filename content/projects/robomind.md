Co-authored RoboMIND, an open multi-embodiment robot dataset and benchmark with official processing utilities, public data, and a paper repository.

## Highlights

- Covers 107K demonstration trajectories, 479 tasks, 96 object categories, and 4 robot embodiments.
- Includes failure trajectories and digital-twin environments for broader policy training and evaluation.
- The official paper repository provides dataset documentation, quick-start guidance, and task resources.
- The open-source utility repository supports dataset quality checking and end-effector pose recalculation.
